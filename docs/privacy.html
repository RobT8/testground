KidRota

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version. KidRota keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no servers of ours. We do not collect, receive, store, see or share any of your information — including your children's names. Nothing you type into KidRota ever reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Who this policy is from

KidRota is an Android app published by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with information, and covers the KidRota app only.

Questions about this policy: support@kidrota.app

What KidRota stores, and where

KidRota stores the plans you create in a database held in the app's own private storage on your device. That includes:

  • The names and colours you give your children
  • The carers you add — names, type, and any cost per day you record
  • Your holidays, their dates and settings
  • Which carer is covering which child on which day, and any notes you add
  • Your app preferences, such as theme and reminder timing

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded, synchronised or backed up to us or to anyone else, and no part of it is transmitted off your phone by the app.

Information about children

KidRota is designed for parents and carers to plan childcare, so you may enter children's first names. We want to be explicit: that information is treated exactly like everything else in the app — it is stored only on your device, we never receive it, and it is deleted when you delete it or uninstall the app.

KidRota is intended for use by adults. It is not directed at children.

What KidRota does not do

  • No user accounts, log-ins or profiles
  • No servers of ours — the app makes no network requests to us
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or advertising
  • No third-party advertising or analytics software is included in the app
  • No selling, renting or sharing of information, because we do not hold any
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera

Permissions the app uses

Notifications

If you turn on holiday reminders, KidRota asks permission to show notifications. These are scheduled and shown entirely by your phone. They are not push notifications, nothing is sent from a server, and we are not told whether a notification was shown or opened.

Sharing a backup file

If you choose to export a backup, KidRota writes a file into its own storage and hands it to your phone's standard share sheet so you can save or send it wherever you like. You decide where it goes. We never receive it. Once that file leaves the app, it is covered by whichever service or app you send it to, not by this policy.

Google Play

KidRota is distributed through Google Play. Google collects certain information in connection with app distribution — for example installation data and, where enabled by your device settings, crash reports. That collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by this one. We may see aggregated, anonymous statistics in the Play Console, such as how many devices have installed the app. That information does not identify you and never includes anything you entered into the app.

Your information, your control

Because everything is on your device, you are in complete control:

  • Delete everything — Settings, then "Delete all data", removes every child, carer, holiday and plan from the device immediately
  • Uninstall — removing the app removes its database with it
  • Export — Settings, then "Export a backup", gives you the whole lot as a readable JSON file

There is no request to make to us and no account to close, because we hold nothing to give you or delete. Under UK GDPR terms, we are not a data controller for anything you enter into KidRota: it never comes into our possession.

Please note that deleting data on your device does not delete any backup file you have already exported and saved elsewhere.

Purchases

KidRota may offer optional paid upgrades in future. Any payment would be handled entirely by Google Play. We would not receive or store your payment details at any point.

Changes to this policy

If KidRota ever changes what it does with information — for example if crash reporting is added in a future version — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
